How to Use FeelFare

Official Mati Fare Calculator

Calculate accurate motorized pedicab & Bao-Bao fares based on official City of Mati distance and fuel tariff regulations.

1

Searching & Dropping Pins

  • Search by Name: Type any destination or barangay (e.g. DOPMC, Baywalk, Matiao) in the search bars and press Enter to pick the top match instantly.
  • Use GPS Location: Tap the crosshair button () on the right of the pickup bar to automatically pin your current location.
  • Interactive Map Tap: Alternatively, click anywhere directly on the map to set your Pickup (Point A) and Destination (Point B).
2

Features & Passenger Options

  • 20% Privilege Discount: Turn on the discount switch for Senior Citizens, Students, and PWDs to apply the mandatory legal discount.
  • Recenter Map Button: If you move or zoom the map, tap the navigation arrow () at the far right of the top distance bar to re-center the route anytime.
3

Tariff Calculation & Changing Fuel Tier

  • How Rates are Calibrated: Fares are based on municipal fuel price tiers (Tiers 1 to 8). Base fare covers the initial 0 to 3 km, with an official fixed rate for every succeeding km.
  • How to Change the Fuel Tier:
    1. Tap the Settings Gear () in the top header. 2. Select the prevailing gasoline price tier (e.g., Tier 1: ₱50.00–₱59.99/L). 3. Click "Save Tariff Rates" to automatically recalibrate all fares!
