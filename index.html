How to use FeelFare

City of Mati Fare Notice

The official fare calculator for motorized pedicabs and Bao-Bao in the City of Mati, based on road distance and the city's fuel tariff.

Sec. 1 Set the trip

  • Search: type a place or barangay (e.g. DOPMC, Baywalk, Matiao) and press Enter to take the top match.
  • My location: tap in the pickup field to use your GPS position as the pickup.
  • Tap the map: the first tap sets the pickup (A), the next sets the destination (B). Drag either pin to adjust.

Sec. 2 Read the notice

  • The stamped figure is the fare to pay. The lines under it show the base fare, the succeeding kilometres and any discount.
  • 20% discount: tick the box for senior citizens, students and PWDs.
  • Recenter: tap in the top bar to bring the whole route back into view.

Sec. 3 Fuel tier

  • Fares follow the city's fuel price tiers (Tier 1 to 8). The base fare covers the first 3 km; each succeeding km adds a fixed rate.
  • The City sets the tier that applies today. To see it, open the menu → Tariff settings.

Sec. 4 Offline use

  • Open it once online. The app saves the city's roads and the map you've viewed, so the fare keeps working without signal.
  • No signal? The route and fare are computed from the saved roads. The distance may differ slightly from the live route.
  • Limits: place search needs internet, so tap the map to set pins. Map areas you haven't viewed stay blank, and pins far from a road can't be routed offline.
